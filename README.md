## THE ESSENCE
SILLAGE is a curated digital encyclopedia meticulously crafted for the olfactory purist. It serves as a sanctuary for those who seek the extraordinary, housing a collection of over 500+ niche and legendary fragrances. Designed with a commitment to minimalist luxury, SILLAGE transcends the traditional database, offering an editorial experience that celebrates the art of scent.

## CURATED FEATURES

### ✧ THE LEGENDARY HOUSES
Explore an expansive library featuring the world’s most prestigious maisons. From the timeless elegance of **Chanel** and **Dior** to the avant-garde complexity of **Xerjoff**, **Roja Parfums**, and **Nishane**. Our collection includes:
- Parfums de Marly
- Tom Ford Private Blend
- Byredo
- Maison Francis Kurkdjian
- Creed

### ✧ THREE-TIER NOTE PYRAMIDS
Experience fragrance structure like never before. Every scent is presented with a responsive, blurred-glass modal visualizer detailing the **Top**, **Heart**, and **Base** notes, allowing you to trace the evolution of a sillage from the first spray to the dry down.

### ✧ GOLD ACCORD INTENSITY
Navigate complexity with our custom **Gold & Amber Intensity Indicators**. These visualizer pills provide an immediate, intuitive understanding of a fragrance’s projection, longevity, and character.

### ✧ LIGHTNING-FAST DISCOVERY
An optimized, debounced search engine ensures that the transition from curiosity to discovery is instantaneous. Navigate the vast catalog with zero friction.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
In a world of visual noise, SILLAGE chooses silence. We have intentionally omitted generic bottle imagery to focus entirely on **Editorial Elegance**. 

The interface is defined by:
- **Flawless Typography:** Utilizing high-contrast serif and sans-serif pairings to evoke the feel of a luxury fashion journal.
- **Atmospheric Palette:** A deep Charcoal base (#0a0a0a) illuminated by sophisticated Amber (#c5a059) accents.
- **Glassmorphism:** Suble, refined transparency layers that create depth without distraction.

## TECHNOLOGICAL STACK
Built for speed and longevity. SILLAGE is engineered with a lightweight, zero-dependency architecture.
- **Core:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3
- **Deployment:** [Netlify](https://perfume-explorer-scoprint.netlify.app)
- **Performance:** Optimized for sub-second load times and smooth motion.
